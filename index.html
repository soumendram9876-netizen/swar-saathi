Features

One app for daily riyaz – drone, rhythm, exercises, raag and checking your sur.

🪕

Tanpura

Real recorded tanpuras (E, C# and more) in all 12 scales and three saptaks. Pa, Ma, Ni and combinations like S, SP, SM, Sn, PnS, gPS, SPR, nS. Adjustable pluck speed.

🎶

Shruti box (surpeti)

Our own surpeti recordings (Sa, Sa-Pa, Sa-Ma). Runs with or without the tanpura, each with its own volume.

🥁

Real tabla

Recorded tabla in Teentaal, Ektaal, Jhaptaal, Rupak, Keherwa, Dadra, Deepchandi, Chautaal, Matta and more – theka, prakar and live styles, in every Sa, 30–300 BPM.

👁️

Bols that follow the tabla

The taal grid (Dha Dhin Dhin Dha …) lights each matra exactly as it is heard, with sam, tali and khali marks.

🎼

Swarmandal

Recorded swarmandal over 3 octaves, tuned to the raag’s swaras. Automatic sweep (Aaroh, Avroh or both), Manual and Semi-automatic.

📝

Alankar library & composer

Built-in alankars, plus a composer with meend, comma (tonguing), khali, repeats [ ], named parts (Sthayi, Antara, taans) and autosave.

🌅

Raag studio

Raag aroh–avroh, pakad and bandish with tabla, a beat-by-beat cursor, your own raags, Join pieces and Print to PDF (A4) in Bhatkhande style.

🎙️

Recordings & voice graph

Record riyaz with the tanpura. A live sur graph shows your voice against the swara lines and how many cents to move up or down.

🎯

Pitch tuner & Know your swara

Tune your bansuri, harmonium or voice; see all 12 swaras of your Sa with their notes and frequencies.

🎹

Keyboard Windows

3-octave swar keyboard (grand piano, harmonium, synth) with octave switch, sustain, and computer-key playing.

🌙

Dark beat screen Android

The screen goes dark and shows only the current matra – easy on the eyes and the battery during long riyaz.

☁️

Backup & share

Save your compositions as a .swar file, keep it in Google Drive, and import it on another phone or PC – or share with your students.

Help

Android says “App not installed”.

Uninstall any older Swar Saathi first (also a Play Store test version), then install the new APK again.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

This appears for new programs downloaded from a website. Click More info and then Run anyway. The Microsoft Store version does not show this.

What happens after 14 days?

The app keeps working: the Practice studio and built-in alankars stay free. To open everything again, enter your licence key in Settings → Unlock.

Will I lose my compositions?

No. Use Backup to save a .swar file (for example to Google Drive) and Import it on any phone or PC.
